## 01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BRAND IDENTITY

### 1.1 Brand Naming Rationale
- **FORNO:** In Italian, *il forno* honors the wood-fired refractory stone kiln hearth—the elemental center of ancient bread-making and pizza gastronomy.
- **27:** Signifies two core milestones:
  1. The **27-hour slow sourdough fermentation** window, where wild yeasts and lactobacilli break down complex starches, generating the distinct Neapolitan blistered leoparding crust, airy crumb structure (*cornicione*), and effortless digestibility.
  2. The founding bottega address at **Via del Forno 27**, Roma.
- **Tagline:** **PIZZA · PANE · BUONA VITA** (Pizza, Bread, Good Living).

### 1.2 Core Brand Positioning
FORNO 27 bridges the gap between traditional Italian pizzeria heritage and contemporary urban lifestyle dining. Rather than competing in the disposable fast-food arena with neon signage and noisy promotional packaging, FORNO 27 operates as an **Artisanal Gastronomy Bottega**, elevating everyday takeaway dining through architectural restraint, sustainable material engineering, and curated pantry extensions.

## 02. DESIGN LANGUAGE: ITALIAN CRAFT × JAPANESE EDITORIAL

The visual system is engineered upon a deliberate synthesis:
1. **Mediterranean Tactile Warmth:**
   - Sun-drenched terracotta brick walls and aged refractory stone.
   - 100% unbleached virgin kraft paperboard with visible natural wood fibers.
   - Olive green herb accents and deep aged wine flexographic inks.
2. **Japanese Editorial Restraint:**
   - Museum-grade whitespace and asymmetric editorial balance.
   - Crisp hairline dividers and precise metadata numbering blocks.
   - Songti / Noto Serif TC and Instrument Serif typographic harmony.
   - Complete rejection of tricolor (red-white-green) clichés and garish advertising banners.

## 04. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY

1. **Display & Heritage (Latin):**
   - **Instrument Serif** (Display 36–130px): Sculpted high-contrast serif with chiseled terminals, echoing historical Roman inscriptions and classical culinary monograph titling.
2. **Editorial Traditional Chinese:**
   - **Noto Serif TC / Songti TC** (13–32px): Refined horizontal-thin, vertical-thick contrast, delivering museum-grade editorial pacing and cultural clarity.
3. **Technical & Digital UI (Latin/Numerical):**
   - **Geist & Geist Mono** (9–14px): Swiss modernist precision, engineered for CAD callouts, packaging dieline coordinates, and interactive digital commerce data.

## 05. 3-TIER PACKAGING & CONCEPT ENGINEERING (V1.1 CORRECTION)

### 5.1 Pizza Box Sizing Architecture (Internal Cavity vs External)
1. **8" Piccola (Personal / Single Feast):**
   - **Internal Food Cavity (內徑):** `215 × 215 × 40 mm` (Internal Volume: `1,850 cm³`)
   - **Exterior Dimensions (外徑):** `220 × 220 × 42 mm`
   - Target Pizza Weight: `~320 g` / Caliper: `1.5 mm E-Flute Corrugated`
2. **12" Classica (Standard Hero / Sharing for Two):**
   - **Internal Food Cavity (內徑):** `314 × 314 × 42 mm` (Internal Volume: `4,140 cm³`)
   - **Exterior Dimensions (外徑):** `320 × 320 × 45 mm`
   - Target Pizza Weight: `~580 g` / Caliper: `1.5 mm E-Flute Corrugated`
3. **16" Famiglia (Family & Party Feast):**
   - **Internal Food Cavity (內徑):** `410 × 410 × 46 mm` (Internal Volume: `7,730 cm³`)
   - **Exterior Dimensions (外徑):** `420 × 420 × 50 mm`
   - Target Pizza Weight: `~950 g` / Caliper: `2.8 mm B-Flute Corrugated`

### 5.2 Takeaway Tote Bag Geometric Footprint Correction
- **Previous Erroneous Spec:** `340 × 330 × 240 mm` (Gusset depth 240 mm physically could not lay two 320 × 320 mm pizza boxes flat).
- **Corrected V1.1 Specification:** **`340 mm (W) × 335 mm (Gusset Depth) × 360 mm (H)`**
- **Geometric Mechanics:** 
  - Bag base area: `340 × 335 mm`, providing `+20 mm` lateral width tolerance and `+15 mm` gusset depth tolerance.
  - Stacking allowance: Two 12" boxes stacked horizontally occupy `320 × 320 × 90 mm` (2 × 45 mm height).
  - Headroom: `270 mm` vertical clearance above pizza stack for handles, thermal air layer, and sauce cup satchels.

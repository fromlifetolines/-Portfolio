FORNO 27

CONTEMPORARY PIZZERIA & BAKERY
Pizza · Pane · Buona Vita

將義大利古老石窯的柴燒火侯與慢速酸種發酵哲學，轉化為當代都會餐飲的精緻日常。本專案以「義式傳統工藝 × 日式雜誌編輯美學」為核心語彙，重塑外帶披薩盒的保溫結構、擴展工藝沾醬與酸種烘焙副線，並架構出涵蓋實體店鋪、工程刀模規格與數位點餐原型的完整原創作品集。 Translating the time-honored artisanal craft of Italian wood-fired brick hearths and slow sourdough fermentation into an elevated contemporary dining ritual. Synthesizing Mediterranean tactile warmth with Japanese editorial restraint, this comprehensive brand ecosystem re-engineers thermal pizza takeaway packaging, curates artisan sauce and bakery extensions, and orchestrates physical spatial touchpoints with a bespoke digital ordering prototype.

CHAPTER 01

The Contemporary Takeaway Challenge

現代外帶餐飲的識別與體驗困境

披薩作為全球最普及的共享美食之一，在過去數十年的商業速食化過程中，往往陷入了同質化的視覺困境：過度飽和的鮮紅與亮黃、嘈雜喧鬧的促銷標籤，以及千篇一律的廉價瓦楞紙箱。更甚者，傳統披薩盒封閉不通風的缺陷，常導致剛出爐的高溫蒸氣凝結為水滴，使精心烘烤的餅皮在 15 分鐘的外送途中變得潮濕軟爛，徹底破壞了料理職人的心血。 While pizza stands as one of the world's most ubiquitous culinary experiences, decades of fast-food commoditization have degraded its visual and tactile dignity. Generic brands drown in screaming hyper-saturated reds and disposable promotional stickers. Crucially, conventional unventilated corrugated boxes trap evaporating steam, condensing moisture back onto the crust and turning artisanal Neapolitan leoparding into soggy dough within a 15-minute delivery window.

FORNO 27 的核心使命：重新定義都會外帶披薩的用餐尊嚴。我們拒絕速食產業的塑料感與廉價印刷，從地中海石窯的陶土暖色、未經漂白的食品級再生紙板，以及嚴密的四角排氣結構著手，讓開箱外帶披薩成為宛如翻閱日式生活風格刊物般純粹、沉靜且具備高度儀式感的體驗。 The Mission of FORNO 27: Reclaiming the culinary dignity of takeaway pizza. Rejecting neon fast-food clichés, we grounded our design language in warm Mediterranean terracotta, unbleached virgin kraft, and engineered moisture ventilation—transforming the takeaway unboxing into a serene, tactile ritual akin to unwrapping a Japanese design monograph.

Brand Strategy & Core Philosophy

Pizza · Pane · Buona Vita

「FORNO」在義大利文中代表烘烤麵包與披薩的「石窯爐」；數字「27」則象徵麵糰經過 27 小時天然酸種低溫發酵的最佳熟成臨界點，也是品牌在羅馬起源處的創始門牌號。標語「Pizza · Pane · Buona Vita」（披薩、烘焙、美好的生活）勾勒出一個不局限於晚餐快餐，而是橫跨晨間佛卡夏、午後工藝麵包與夜晚窯烤聚餐的全日餐飲生活願景。 In Italian, "FORNO" honors the traditional refractory stone kiln hearth; "27" signifies the 27-hour low-temperature sourdough fermentation window where wild yeasts unlock peak aromatic depth and digestibility, as well as the heritage studio address. Our motto, "Pizza · Pane · Buona Vita", expands the brand from an evening takeaway into an all-day artisanal bakery and culinary lifestyle destination.

視覺平衡點：捨棄義大利餐飲常見的紅白綠聖誕配色，轉而汲取南歐托斯卡尼陶土磚、橄欖樹葉與熟成紅酒的自然色素；版面排印導入當代日式編輯刊物的留白控制、細髮絲分隔線與嚴謹的雙語秩序，呈現兼具歷史溫度與理性結構的現代美感。 Aesthetic Equilibrium: Moving away from predictable tricolor clichés, our palette draws upon sun-baked Tuscan terracotta tiles, wild olive foliage, and deep aged wine. Layouts borrow from Japanese editorial publications: hair-line rules, expansive breathing room, and meticulous bilingual alignment.

CHAPTER 03

Locked Brand Identity & 9-Brick Radial Arch

正式品牌母稿：九石放射窯拱識別體系

經與 18 張實體攝影作品嚴格比對，品牌母稿精確錨定於「九石放射石窯拱門（9-Brick Radial Kiln Arch）」造型。九塊幾何砌石精準呼應義式石窯的拱頂砌築工藝，中心拱頂石居中平衡，結合雕刻感古典襯線字體，達成與既有照片 100% 絕對一致的視覺純度。 Rigorously aligned with the physical photography assets across all 18 shots, the locked brand identity is anchored upon the 9-Brick Radial Kiln Arch. Nine precision keystone blocks honor classical Italian masonry hearth construction, paired with sculpted high-contrast serif typography.

核心豎式標誌：九石拱門居中，下方搭配 FORNO 27 字標與「PIZZA · PANE · BUONA VITA」標語，用於披薩盒蓋中央主視覺與店鋪封印。 Core Vertical Lockup: Radial arch atop FORNO 27 wordmark and tagline, applied as the primary pizza box hero stamp.

CHAPTER 04

Complete Hospitality Product Family

完整餐飲產品線：三規格披薩盒、工藝醬料與烘焙副線

FORNO 27 不僅是一間披薩店，更是一座完整的現代烘焙與餐飲工藝體系。從單人享用的 8 吋個人盒、12 吋標準盒至 16 吋家庭分享盒，延伸出自家熬製的義式沾醬與天然酸種麵包系列。 FORNO 27 encompasses a complete product-line hospitality ecosystem. From personal 8-inch boxes to 12-inch standard and 16-inch family feasts, complemented by bespoke pantry dipping sauces and long-fermented sourdough hearth breads.

Pane & Lievito Madre

藉由共用石窯爐高溫與老麵酵母（Lievito Madre），FORNO 27 延伸出晨間烘焙副線。迷迭香佛卡夏、拖鞋麵包（Ciabatta）與手揉義式脆餅（Grissini），讓門市在非披薩尖峰時段維持充沛的香氣與零售營收。 Leveraging the thermal inertia of refractory stone ovens and heritage wild starter yeasts (Lievito Madre), the bakery line provides all-day commercial vibrancy. Sea-salt focaccia, crusty ciabatta, and hand-rolled grissini enrich brand depth well beyond standard pizza operating hours.

在外帶包裝的物理防護上，FORNO 27 採用微細 E 楞（E-Flute 1.5mm）與 B 楞（B-Flute 2.8mm）再生瓦楞芯紙。箱內配置食品級水性防油塗層（KIT 8+），完全杜絕氟化物（PFAS-Free），並透過四角斜切微型排氣孔，精準平衡「保溫蓄熱（維持核心 62°C 以上達 25 分鐘）」與「排導高溫蒸氣（避免餅皮受潮變軟）」兩項極端物理條件。 Packaging engineering utilizes precision micro-flute corrugated structures (1.5mm E-Flute / 2.8mm B-Flute). Coated internally with a non-toxic aqueous grease-resistant barrier (KIT 8+, PFAS-free), the box integrates 4 laser-cut corner vents. This balances thermal heat retention (keeping pizzas above 62°C for 25 minutes) with active vapor release to preserve crispy crust texture.

CHAPTER 08

Digital Ordering Experience & Responsive UI

數位點餐系統與響應式互動原型 (SHOT 17)

現代餐飲品牌不可或缺的數位介面。我們以乾淨清晰的義式當代字體排印，架構出支援桌機與 390px 手機檢視的完整原生互動點餐介面。包含披薩尺寸選配、薄厚餅皮設定、工藝沾醬加購、購物車即時連動與外帶預估時間計算。 An essential digital capability for modern hospitality. Designed with responsive precision, this interactive ordering prototype supports desktop and 390px mobile viewports—featuring pizza size selection, crust customization, sauce add-ons, and real-time cart calculations.

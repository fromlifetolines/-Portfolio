STRUCTURAL & THERMAL DESIGN INTENT

  • Thermal Heat Retention Target: Micro-flute corrugated dead-air pocket designed to buffer heat dissipation during estimated 20–25 min transit.
  • Moisture Relief Ventilation: 4× triangular laser-cut vents at rear hinges release moisture vapor while minimizing heat loss.
  • Adhesive-Free Glueless Fold: Precision mechanical corner tabs lock rigidly without chemical adhesive near hot food steam.
  • Water-Based Barrier Target: Proposed inner food-grade aqueous barrier coating to resist oil and moisture penetration without fluorochemicals.
  • Stackability Target: Structural design intended for 2-box bag delivery and up to 4-box staging without corner collapse.

CONCEPT BILL OF MATERIALS (BOM)

  • Exterior Liner: Virgin unbleached kraft paperboard (Paper Cream tone).
  • Fluting Medium: Recycled corrugated core fluting (Wave E: 1.5mm / Wave B: 2.8mm).
  • Interior Liner: Food-grade grease-resistant kraft with aqueous barrier.
  • Flexo Printing Ink: Water-based non-toxic soy ink (2 Spot Colors: Forno Wine #8C2D26 & Deep Ink #251C19).

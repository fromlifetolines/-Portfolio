PORTFOLIO 11 / ARCHITECTURAL CULTURE PUBLISHER

空間的閱讀方式，
與時間留下的痕跡。 Reading Space,
and the Marks of Time.

CROSS SECTION 是以建築文化為題的出版識別與數位閱讀設計。完成雙期刊物封面、六篇中英專文編輯排版、出版應用視覺，以及具備搜尋、收藏與字級調整的閱讀原型。

CROSS SECTION is an architectural culture publishing identity and digital reading design project, including two issue covers, six bilingual editorial layouts, publication applications and a functional reading prototype with search, bookmarks and adjustable text size.

設計註記（Project Notes） Project Notes

CROSS SECTION 為 HOWARD HUANG 個人作品集第 11 件獨立設計提案（Independent Design Project）。所有刊物名稱、專題文章、作者與圖說皆為原創示範文本；展示出版視覺提案與數位閱讀體驗，非實體發行刊物，不涉及真實期刊發行、採訪授權、訂閱收費或第三方背書。

CROSS SECTION is an independent design project in Howard Huang's portfolio. All publication titles, essays, and visual plates are original design demonstration content. Physical issue visuals are design proposals rather than mass-market distributed publications.

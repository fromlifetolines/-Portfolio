PROJECT BRIEF & SPATIAL PLANNING

沉靜秩序，
包容人與品牌的靈感交會。

Quiet order,
accommodating brands and people.

在當代都會稠密的建築語境中，LINEAR SPACE 探索如何以極簡幾何、質樸石材與精準光影，創造一處能讓人放慢呼吸、專注於材質與對話的 180 m² 空間容器。

In dense urban density, LINEAR SPACE explores how pure geometric volumes, understated stone, and calibrated light can create a 180 m² spatial container that slows visitors down and invites deep engagement.

空間是品牌的實體延伸。 本案以都會展演藝廊與設計交流沙龍為定位，摒棄過度裝飾的商業陳設，回歸建築純粹的面、線與體量。透過挑高天光與內斂立面，使展示品與參觀者成為空間的主角。

Space is the tangible extension of brand culture. Conceived as an urban gallery and design salon, this concept rejects gratuitous ornament to celebrate architectural planes, lines, and proportion. Controlled natural light and restrained vertical surfaces allow both objects and people to take center stage.

180 m² 精準動線佈局。 依據使用功能劃分為五個相互呼應的區域，從開放迎賓、集中展演、親密交流到私密諮詢與高效後勤，層層遞進，確保空間流通與隱私需求的完美平衡。

180 m² precise circulation. The floorplate is organized into five complementary zones—from welcoming arrival and expansive gallery to intimate conversation, private consultation, and discreet backstage service—balancing public porosity with acoustic intimacy.

04 / ENVIRONMENTAL GRAPHICS & DIGITAL UX

實體環境導視，
與自適應數位體驗的交會。

Signage systems meeting
responsive digital experiences.

設計不僅停留在牆體與地面，更延伸至訪客前行中的每一個識別指引與手中的數位互動。

Design extends beyond stone and timber to every wayfinding touchpoint and interactive digital handheld experience.

WAYFINDING & SIGNAGE (SHOT 09)

建築幾何環境導視系統

Architectural Wayfinding & Signage

導視不只是指示牌，而是建築表皮的一部分。採用霧黑金屬板鐫刻微型字體與俐落箭頭，在保持極簡美學的同時，提供清晰無負擔的路徑指引。

Wayfinding as an organic layer of architecture. Matte black metal panels engraved with precise typographic scales and clean arrows provide intuitive direction without visual noise.

CUSTOM FURNITURE (SHOT 10)

沉靜木作與石材收邊工藝

Quiet Joinery & Stone Detailing

為沙龍與藝廊量身打造的展陳島台。石材邊角施以精密 45 度倒角，與煙燻橡木接縫處以 3mm 陰刻勾縫收邊，展示沉靜家具概念（非實際施工工程圖）。

Custom exhibition plinths tailored for the gallery. Stone surfaces feature precision 45-degree chamfers, joined to smoked oak with 3mm shadow gaps (schematic furniture concept, not fabrication engineering).

DIGITAL EXTENSION (SHOT 13)

沉浸式桌機展演網站

Immersive Desktop Web Experience

將實體建築概念轉化為數位線上平台。以暗調背景、優雅非對稱網格與高畫質影像，讓遠端訪客亦能感受 FRAME/01 的空間比例與光影流動。

Translating physical space into digital architecture. Dark-mode canvas, refined asymmetric grids, and rich imagery allow global audiences to experience the proportions and lighting of FRAME/01.

MOBILE UX (SHOT 14)

掌中空間導覽與選品預約

Mobile Spatial Companion

針對行動裝置優化的響應式介面。流暢的觸控滑動、清晰的區域切換與直覺的預約導覽體驗，打造實體與虛擬無縫銜接的當代參訪流程。

A tailored responsive companion designed for mobile devices. Fluid gesture navigation and clean zone switching create an effortless bridge between digital planning and physical visitation.

05 / PROJECT GOVERNANCE & FACTUAL DISCLOSURE

自主概念研究聲明

Concept Study Disclaimer

本專案（LINEAR SPACE / FRAME/01）為設計師 Howard Huang 與 FLTL 自主發起之建築概念、空間規劃與品牌環境識別設計提案。非實際商業委託案，未取得建築執照、消防審查、節能綠建築認證或工程施工圖簽證。所有圖面、面積（180 m²）與空間配置皆屬設計概念研究範疇。

This project (LINEAR SPACE / FRAME/01) is a self-initiated architectural concept and brand environment study authored by designer Howard Huang and FLTL. It is not a commercial commission, and has not undergone building permitting, structural sign-off, fire safety review, or green building certification. All drawings, dimensions (180 m²), and zoning are conceptual proposals.

影像與技術揭露

Rendering & Technical Truth

全套 16 幕設計圖板均以 2560 × 1440 最終畫布排版輸出。部分原始攝影元素源自 1672 × 941 高階生成影像並經重採樣合成，絕無虛假宣稱原生 4K 實景攝影。SHOT 11 之人物動線視覺化為動態與尺度概念示意，非竣工實景之真實訪客攝影；SHOT 10 家具木作為概念接合研究，非實際施工工程圖。本網站所有互動元件均為純前端記憶體示範，無任何個人資料收集、真實預約或線上金流交易行為。

All 16 design plates are composed on 2560 × 1440 master canvases. Select photography elements originate from 1672 × 941 native AI generation resampled with Lanczos filtering; no claim of native 4K photography is made. SHOT 11 human circulation silhouettes represent conceptual movement visualizations, not physical visitor photography at a completed project. SHOT 10 furniture joinery is schematic concept design, not fabrication engineering. All website interactives are client-side demonstrations without data collection, live bookings, or monetary transactions.

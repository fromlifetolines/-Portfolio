Honest Concept Disclaimer: ATLAS OPS is an independent exploratory concept study. All equipment, apparel, retail environments, and campaign assets are design prototypes. In-image terminology such as 'Weatherproof' or 'Field Tested' represents conceptual art direction rather than certified lab performance. Topographic elevations and coordinates are artistic styling elements. Digital interfaces are mockups with zero commercial transactions or active inventory.

品牌識別與視覺基礎 IDENTITY & GRAPHIC FOUNDATION

以 60 度幾何標誌、高對比標準字與等高線資訊網格，奠定硬派山岳品牌秩序。 Establishing rugged alpine discipline through geometric 'A' emblem geometry, high-contrast type architecture, and topographic info grids.

1672 × 941 RAW
SHOT 02 / 16

LOGO & IDENTITY SYSTEM

標誌與識別：幾何 A 的構成、留白和不同背景上的應用方式。以 60 度斜角切面與折疊負空間象徵山岳峰稜，作為全品牌唯一且權威的標準視覺基準（Standard Benchmark）。

Logo & Identity System: Geometric 'A' emblem construction, 60-degree angled facets, and negative-space fold geometry. Serves as the authoritative visual anchor for the brand.

1672 × 941 RAW
SHOT 03 / 16

TYPE SYSTEM

字體系統：大寫字標搭配等寬資訊字，處理標題與裝備資訊的閱讀層級。大標題採用極度厚實的寬體大寫字母表（A–Z 與 0–9 完整呈現），技術規格則以等寬字元保持資料嚴謹度。本專案字體為排版概念研究，非對外發行之實體字型檔。

Type System: Extended bold display titles paired with mono technical information grids for dual-tier hierarchy. Clean A–Z and 0–9 alphanumeric sets structure tabular gear dossiers. Typographic systems represent layout design studies rather than custom commercial font releases.

1672 × 941 RAW
SHOT 04 / 16

COLOR & GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

色彩與圖形：五種核心品牌色搭配等高線，讓地形語彙延伸至識別設計。炭黑（Charcoal #0B0B0B）、岩板灰（Slate #2D3238）、礫石灰（Stone #8E959C）、雪地白（Snow #E6E6E6）四階無彩色中，注入警戒橘（Alert Orange #FF5A1F）作為高辨識度安全指示。向量等高線標註為排版視覺符號，非實際登山地形圖。

Color & Graphic Language: Charcoal (#0B0B0B), Slate (#2D3238), Stone (#8E959C), and Snow (#E6E6E6) grayscale values accented by Alert Orange (#FF5A1F). Vector contour lines serve as graphic identity elements rather than certified navigational topography.

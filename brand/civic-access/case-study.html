PORTFOLIO 12 / PUBLIC SERVICE DESIGN & ACCESSIBLE UX

讓公共服務，
更容易被理解與完成。 Make every next step clear.

CIVIC ACCESS 以地方公共服務窗口的常見申辦情境為設計命題，重新規劃服務探索、申請身分判斷、文件準備、表單填寫與進度查詢的體驗。視覺系統延伸至紙本服務指引與現場導視，讓不同需求的使用者都能理解資訊，知道下一步該怎麼做。

CIVIC ACCESS rethinks the experience of discovering public services, identifying application scenarios, preparing documents, completing forms and understanding application status. A unified visual language extends into printed guidance and on-site wayfinding, making complex service information easier to navigate across digital and physical touchpoints.

03 / PROJECT BRIEF

專案命題與設計挑戰 Project Brief & Challenges

公共服務常同時提供線上申辦與臨櫃協助，但服務分類、申請條件與文件準備方式容易分散在不同頁面。初次申請者必須自行判斷符合哪一類服務；需要代辦或輔助操作的使用者，也可能在過程中遇到額外阻礙。

本案採用以使用者任務為中心的服務設計方法，整合數位入口、表單流程與現場服務接觸點，建立可重複使用的設計規範與原型架構。

Public-facing services often involve multiple channels, application requirements and document lists. First-time applicants must determine which service fits their needs, while caregivers or people using assistive technology may encounter additional friction along the way.

This design study develops a user-centered framework for service discovery, guided applications and assisted access. Its purpose is to demonstrate service design and interface planning, not to represent an active government service.

04 / DESIGN RESPONSE

五大設計回應策略 Five Design Response Pillars

01 服務資訊架構： 將常見需求整理成易理解的分類，以搜尋、申辦指南及協助入口，減少使用者在不同頁面尋找資訊的負擔。 01 Service Architecture: Clear categories, search and contextual guidance help people locate relevant services without cognitive friction.
02 情境式資格引導： 先確認本人辦理或協助代辦，再呈現相對應的示範文件清單，避免填寫到一半才發現資料不足。資格規則僅供流程設計展示，不代表真實法規認定。 02 Scenario-based Guidance: Applicant and representative paths reveal different sample document requirements before form completion.
03 可修復的表單體驗： 表單依任務分段，保留返回修改的能力，並於欄位附近明確說明錯誤原因與修正方法。 03 Recoverable Forms: Progressive steps and precise validation messages make errors easier to locate and correct without data loss.
04 跨接觸點識別： 以 Civic Blue 與高可讀性的文字階層，統一數位服務、紙本手冊、自助申辦機及服務中心導視的視覺語言。 04 Cross-channel Identity: A consistent visual language connects the digital portal, printed guidance, service kiosk and environmental signage.
05 無障礙設計規範： 針對字級、對比、鍵盤焦點、互動目標與狀態通知建立原型驗收項目。符合性須經實際瀏覽器及輔助科技測試確認，不能以圖版作為認證證據。 05 Accessible Design Specifications: Typography, contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation and status communication are defined as implementation and testing requirements.

05 / DESIGN EVIDENCE & VALUE

設計成果與商業價值 Design Evidence & Commercial Value

本次視覺交付涵蓋品牌識別、公共服務中心應用、紙本申辦手冊、服務藍圖、桌機及行動介面、身分與文件分支、表單驗證、錯誤修復、附件狀態、示範案件追蹤及無障礙驗收規範。這件作品展示如何把多角色參與的行政服務，整理成可理解的資訊架構與可維護的介面系統，也說明品牌設計如何延伸到現場服務與紙本溝通。

不宣稱實測完成率、節省時間百分比、使用者滿意度或真實公部門導入成果。 後續若進入實際開發與測試，可衡量任務完成率、第一次送出錯誤率、缺件比例、錯誤修復時間，以及使用輔助科技完成任務的情況。

The case includes visual identity, environmental applications, printed materials, service blueprints, desktop and mobile interfaces, conditional document checklists, form validation states, upload flows, status tracking and an accessibility validation plan. Interactive behavior is implemented and verified directly in the client browser.

This work demonstrates a practical approach to service design for public-facing organizations, social-service providers, healthcare administration and complex enterprise workflows. No real client engagement, measured business outcome or official government endorsement is claimed.

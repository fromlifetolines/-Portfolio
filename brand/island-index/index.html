島序

ISLAND INDEX — A Contemporary Taiwanese Tea Atlas

以地理序列（Geographic Sequence）為經緯，重新編排台灣山巒間的微型氣候與製茶工藝。本專案融合當代日式編輯設計的版面秩序與瑞士字體排印學的精密理性，為四個指標性產地構建出兼具高辨識度、文化深度與商業擴充性的高級茶葉品牌家族。 Structuring Taiwan’s mountain microclimates and artisan tea craftsmanship through a rigorous geographic sequence. Synthesizing Japanese editorial layout sensibility with Swiss typographic discipline, this comprehensive brand and packaging system delivers high distinctiveness, cultural resonance, and commercial scalability across four signature terroirs.

CHAPTER 01

品牌策略與命名考究

Brand Strategy & Naming Rationale

台灣茶葉擁有世界上最密集的山脈微氣候群與最精湛的半發酵烏龍工藝，然而傳統市場往往受限於兩極化的視覺樣貌：一類是過度繁複、沉重且難以辨識的傳統書法字體；另一類則是捨棄所有產地脈絡的極簡扁平包裝。 Taiwan boasts the world's most concentrated microclimate diversity and artisanal semi-oxidized oolong craftsmanship. Yet traditional tea branding has long been polarized between heavy, illegible calligraphy and generic minimalist packaging that discards all geographical narrative.

「島序 ISLAND INDEX」的誕生旨在建立一套當代茶葉風土索引系統（Contemporary Taiwanese Tea Atlas）。「島」代表台灣這座由板塊造山運動形塑的島嶼；「序」既是地理與海拔的編排次序（Sequence），也是百科全書般的品飲指南（Index）。我們將產地氣候、海拔等高線、發酵度與焙火程度化為嚴謹的視覺序列，讓品茗成為一場由低海拔丘陵通往中央山脈雲霧之巔的感官旅行。 ISLAND INDEX was conceived as a contemporary atlas of Taiwanese tea terroir. "Island" (島) honors the dramatic geological terrain formed by plate tectonics; "Index" (序) embodies both a geographic sequence of elevation and an encyclopedia of taste. By transforming microclimates, contour lines, oxidation, and roasting parameters into an intuitive visual indexing language, every cup becomes an orderly sensory expedition.

設計決策：Direction A × Direction C 融合

在 Phase 1 的三組概念中，Direction A（地理序列）具備最強大的多 SKU 擴充邏輯與地圖等高線符號；而 Direction C（雜誌字體排印）則展現極高的日式編輯留白與排印精緻度。本案最終選用 Editorial Geo Atlas（編輯風土圖誌） 進行精修，捨棄傳統書法，改以原創修整的明體橫豎筆畫，搭配 Instrument Serif 英文襯線字體，達成高級編輯刊物般的氣度。 Phase 1 evaluated three distinct routes. Direction A offered the strongest multi-SKU systematic indexing, while Direction C provided unmatched Japanese editorial whitespace and typography. The final design unifies both into the Editorial Geo Atlas: custom optically-balanced Songti lettering paired with Instrument Serif, capturing the sophistication of a museum monograph.

CHAPTER 02

品牌識別母稿與符號規範

Master Vector Identity & Typographic Architecture

所有的標誌與標準字均為 100% 獨立設計的向量母稿，並經過微觀視角光學修正。中文標準字「島序」強化了橫細豎粗的對比，收筆處帶有俐落的刀鋒切角；英文標準字「ISLAND INDEX」則拉開字母間距，在深色印刷、燙金與極小尺寸下依然保有極高的骨架清晰度。 All logos and wordmarks are 100% bespoke authored vector master assets subjected to rigorous optical balancing. The Chinese wordmark "島序" features sharp chiseled terminals with balanced stroke weight; the Latin counterpart "ISLAND INDEX" utilizes expansive tracking to maintain pristine structural legibility under micro-scale embossing and metallic hot foil stamping.

CHAPTER 03

四產地茶品矩陣與數據合規

Four-SKU Terroir Architecture & Rigorous Data Integrity

本專案嚴格遵守誠信原則，杜絕虛構未經驗證的農場經緯度、非客觀測量的實驗室發酵率或捏造的競賽得獎認證。所有四款茶品數據均標示為概念規格（Concept Specification），並忠實反映台灣真實茶區的經典風土特色。 Strict adherence to professional design ethics: zero fabricated farm GPS coordinates, no false laboratory precision, and zero fictitious competition awards. All four product profiles are clearly designated as Concept Specifications, authentically honoring Taiwan’s true regional cultivars and artisanal styles.

CHAPTER 04

結構包裝系統與工程刀模

Packaging Architecture, Materiality & Engineering Dielines

在包裝工程上，本系統採用高度模組化的幾何共用容器架構。四款茶品共用統一的 W85 × D85 × H140mm 輕量挺度紙盒外裝，內置高密封防光金屬拉絲茶罐。透過特選未塗布日本和紙質感厚卡、深壓凹等高線紋理（Blind Deboss）、金屬溫銅燙金（Copper Foil）與撕裂封籤結構，達成極具觸覺記憶度的開箱體驗。 The packaging architecture implements a modular, shared geometric container system. All four SKUs utilize a standardized W85 × D85 × H140mm rigid outer carton housing an airtight brushed aluminum tin canister. Tactile memory is achieved through uncoated Japanese cotton board, blind-debossed contour topography, warm copper hot foil stamping, and tamper-evident archival sealing strips.

CHAPTER 05

四款產地品飲敘事與茶具光影

Four Tea Product Stories & Brewing Aesthetics

品飲視覺以真實呈現茶葉型態（乾茶球型與條索）、專屬茶器配置與茶湯原色為核心原則。阿里山的清碧透亮、峨眉美人茶的琥珀明艷、日月潭紅玉的寶石紅潤、以及凍頂老烏龍的澄金油潤，各自對應了精確的器物材質與光影氛圍。 Visual storytelling is strictly grounded in authentic leaf morphology (tightly rolled oolong spheres vs. twisted tips), tailored ceramic vessels, and genuine liquor color. The crystal jade of Alishan, radiant amber of Oriental Beauty, deep ruby of Sun Moon Lake, and warm golden oiliness of roasted Dongding are each paired with distinct light, surface textures, and teaware.

CHAPTER 07

專案總結與未來品牌管線標準

Execution Summary & Reusable Architecture

「島序 ISLAND INDEX」的完成，驗證了一套高標準、高自律且兼具商業落地可行性的完整品牌建構管線。從最初的策略命名、字體工藝、四產地數據合規、包裝刀模工程、18 張全視角實景 Mockup 渲染合成，到這份採用日式設計年鑑排版規範的雙語案例網頁，全程貫徹「向量母稿嚴格映射、零虛構 AI 文字、零破碎版面」的專業設計準則。 The realization of ISLAND INDEX proves an autonomous, commercially rigorous brand production pipeline. From naming strategy and custom typography, to data compliance audits, structural dieline engineering, 18 perspective-accurate photographic composite mockups, and this bilingual editorial case study, the project upholds a steadfast principle: 100% authored vector typography, zero synthetic AI gibberish, and zero compromised layouts.

本案例建立的 12 欄響應式網格系統、持久化雙語切換引擎、全螢幕燈箱檢查機制與模組化刀模規範，將直接作為 Howard / FLTL 作品集後續原創品牌（如 FORNO 27 義式柴燒烘焙 與 FORME LAB 模組化美妝保養）的高效生產模板。 The 12-column responsive layout, persistent bilingual engine, fullscreen inspection lightbox, and standardized dieline framework established here will serve as the core production architecture for forthcoming original brands in Howard / FLTL's portfolio, including FORNO 27 Artisanal Bakery and FORME LAB Modular Skincare.

CHAPTER 01 // BRAND FOUNDATION & USER CONTEXT

多重專業角色與嚴謹資訊架構 Multi-Role User Context & Information Architecture

B2B 採購決策絕非單一衝動消費，而是包含採購經辦、工程師與審批主管的跨層級流程。透過角色矩陣與日式型錄排版，讓技術數據成為最高信任基礎。

Industrial B2B procurement involves multi-stakeholder workflows spanning buyers, engineers, and financial approvers with zero tolerance for ambiguity.

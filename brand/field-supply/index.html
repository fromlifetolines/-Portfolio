PORTFOLIO 10 / MULTI-BRAND OUTDOOR RETAIL

選品與規格，

FIELD SUPPLY 以多品牌戶外零售為核心，規劃品牌識別、商品陳列視覺、印刷型錄與電商操作體驗。網站原型整合八件示範商品、跨品牌規格比較、服飾尺碼選擇、運費試算、三階段訪客結帳及售後流程展示。

FIELD SUPPLY explores a multi-brand outdoor retailer through a complete visual identity, merchandise catalog, retail communication system, and functional e-commerce prototype. The prototype integrates eight sample products, cross-brand specification comparisons, apparel sizing, shipping calculation, a three-stage guest checkout, and aftercare flows.

This case demonstrates the product information architecture and interactive e-commerce flow for eight outdoor products. Pricing and shipping rules are for interface evaluation; guest checkout, order tracking, and aftercare workflows are interactive demonstrations without live payments or carrier integration.

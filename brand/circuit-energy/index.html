PROJECT BRIEF & BRAND STRATEGY

流動的能源迴路，
連繫自然地貌與現代生活。

Continuous energy circuits,
connecting landscapes with modern life.

當能源轉型邁向深度整合，綠能基礎建設不再只是冰冷的工廠，而是能與山林、水文共生，並對大眾開放的永續文化地標。CIRCUIT ENERGY 探索如何透過整合性設計，賦予清潔能源清晰、高質感且具信賴感的當代樣貌。

As clean energy transitions toward deep decentralization, infrastructure transcends functional utilities into ecological landmarks harmonizing with natural topography and welcoming public literacy.

永續需要可感知的設計承載。 許多綠能提案常陷於生硬的工程術語或低質感的宣傳文宣。本案從品牌識別（Visual Identity）、景觀建築（Landscape Architecture）到數位監控體驗（Digital Telemetry），建立一套極簡、深邃而具科技溫度的全域設計系統。

Sustainability demands intuitive, tactile design. Clean energy proposals often default to dry engineering jargon or generic clip-art. CIRCUIT ENERGY builds a unified design language—from typography and mass timber architecture to dark-mode telemetry—delivering authority and emotional resonance.

三大核心發電與調度支柱。 園區整合高效雙面光伏、低切入風速風機與模組化液冷儲能系統，透過智慧微電網演算法平抑天候間歇性，達成發電與負載的動態平衡。

Triad of generation and storage pillars. Combining bifacial solar arrays, low cut-in wind turbines, and modular liquid-cooled BESS containers, the concept smooths out intermittency through algorithmic dispatch.

01

循環共生哲學 (Circularity)

Circularity Philosophy

以不斷流轉的能量之環為象徵，強調整體生命週期的材料回收與低碳足跡。

Continuous energy loops symbolizing closed lifecycle stewardship and restrained carbon footprints.

02

低環境衝擊地貌建築 (Low-Impact Architecture)

Low-Impact Architecture

建築順應山勢與水岸起伏，運用在地木料與低眩光材質，讓設施自然融於大地。

Facilities contoured into terrain using locally sourced timber and non-reflective coatings.

Transparent Telemetry

將複雜的千瓦時與調度功率轉化為直覺的數位圖表，落實數據治理與公眾知情權。

Complex energy flows translated into intuitive dashboards, promoting scientific clarity.

專案性質說明

Project Scope

本專案（CIRCUIT ENERGY / PORTFOLIO 09）為綠能示範園區之獨立品牌識別、空間規劃與數位產品介面設計提案。非實際商業委託案，未獲政府公共工程特許、電業執照或施工許可。圖面中所有建築、發電陣列與儲能站點皆屬設計提案範疇。

This project (CIRCUIT ENERGY / PORTFOLIO 09) is an independent renewable energy campus brand identity, spatial environment, and digital UX design proposal. It is not an active commercial commission, utility concession, or permitted project. All facilities, systems, and zoning are design proposals.

數據模型與示範說明

Data Model & Demonstration Metrics

美術圖版（SHOT 07 與 SHOT 14）中所顯示之「328.5 MW」為介面概念設計中的版面示範數字；MW 屬於功率（發電速率）單位而非發電總量（MWh）。圖版提及之「125K t 減碳」、「320 GWh」、「85K 家戶」等均為示範情境數據，非經第三方審計之實測結果。SHOT 10 雙面太陽能板爆炸圖為工業設計美學展示，非施工製造圖。

Visual plates (SHOT 07 & 14) feature "328.5 MW" as an illustrative UI mockup figure; MW represents power capacity, not cumulative energy (MWh). Metrics including "125K t CO2", "320 GWh", and "85K households" are illustrative demonstration figures, not audited ESG outcomes. SHOT 10 PV exploded view represents a schematic styling study, not fabrication engineering.

影像生成與解析度真實性

Rendering Truth & Canvas Resolution

全套 16 幕展示圖板均以 2560 × 1440 最終展示畫布輸出。其原始影像素材源自 1672 × 941 高階生成影像，並經由高品質 Lanczos 演算法重採樣至 2560 × 1440 畫布，非原生 4K 光學實景拍攝。圖檔元資料中之 300 DPI 僅代表列印解析度設定。

All 16 design plates are composed on 2560 × 1440 presentation canvases. The source visual elements originate from 1672 × 941 native AI-generated imagery and were Lanczos-resampled to 2560 × 1440 without artwork modification. They are not native 4K photographs. 300 DPI metadata reflects print container defaults.

無追蹤與安全防護承諾

Privacy & Zero-Tracking Guarantee

All interactive modules on this website (system selector, energy simulator, master lightbox) run strictly client-side in memory without external telemetry, tracking scripts, user registration, or financial transactions.

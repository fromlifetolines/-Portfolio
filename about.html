WHO I AM

Howard Huang is a Creative Strategist, Visual Design Director, and Digital Product Builder with more than a decade of cross-disciplinary experience across design, advertising, and digital experience.

Operating at the critical intersection where brand narrative meets digital architecture, Howard leads creative systems that transform strategic goals into unmistakable visual identities and responsive products.

With 10+ years directing visual design and digital craft, he works as both a strategic leader and hands-on builder—designing cohesive brand universes, high-conversion interfaces, and tactile physical-digital touchpoints.

MY CREATIVE PRACTICE

“It doesn’t matter where you start, it’s how you craft the systems that endure.”

Howard’s creative practice is defined by systematic rigour and aesthetic uncompromisingness. Rather than treating design as decorative styling, every project begins with structural thinking: establishing the brand’s conceptual core, typography hierarchy, behavioral logic, and tactile materiality.

From editorial art direction and high-contrast typography to interactive 3D WebGL objects and performance-tuned frontend systems, the practice continuously bridges physical craftsmanship with modern web capabilities.

04

FROM LIFE TO LINES

“From Life to Lines” (FLTL) is Howard’s self-initiated design philosophy and product platform.

It represents the translation of life’s spontaneous interactions into precise, enduring design lines. The philosophy asserts that modern design must not remain trapped behind screens—it should live in the physical hands of people, connecting physical presence with seamless digital identity.

The flagship realization of this platform is the FLTL NFC Smart Business Card (2026): a premium matte-black PVC card engineered with an embedded NTAG216 micro-chip, supporting instantaneous contact sharing across iOS and Android without any application installation.
